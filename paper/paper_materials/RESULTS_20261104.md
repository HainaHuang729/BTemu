## 3. PS + LF joint chain (exploratory, not authorised)

- Configuration: fixed KP=1, MS=1.5, 6-D astrophysical, LF (34 points) + 180-cell PS with sigma = sqrt(SEM^2 + (0.10 P)^2).
- Outcome: the chain runs to the prior corners (ALPHA_STAR→1.0, M_TURN→10, F_ESC10→−3.0, t_STAR→0.02).
- Diagnostic: at the data's own theta the emulator reproduces the PS with **chi2 = 5.81 over 180 cells** (median fractional error 1.28%, q90 2.9%). So the PS term is nearly perfect at the truth yet the chain does not stay there — the sampler is being pushed by something other than the PS residual (candidate causes: theta→astro mapping in the sampler, or −inf rejections around the truth from the phase53-prior intersection / LF-grid check).
