## 3. PS + LF joint chain (exploratory, not authorised)

- Configuration: fixed KP=1, MS=1.5, 6-D astrophysical, LF (34 points) + 180-cell PS with sigma = sqrt(SEM^2 + (0.10 P)^2).
- Outcome: the chain runs to the prior corners (ALPHA_STAR→1.0, M_TURN→10, F_ESC10→−3.0, t_STAR→0.02).
- Diagnostic: at the data's own theta the emulator reproduces the PS with **chi2 = 5.81 over 180 cells** (median fractional error 1.28%, q90 2.9%). This is one reported PS check at the injected parameters; it does not isolate the cause of the chain behaviour. Candidate checks are the theta→astro mapping, prior support, LF-grid validity and each likelihood contribution at both the injection and the corner.
