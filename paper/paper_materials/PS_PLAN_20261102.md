# Proposed PS extension: targets, pilot measurements and conditional cost arithmetic

Revised 2026-11-03. This is a proposal; no simulation, training, GPU request or sampler is launched by this document. The previous November 2 estimates are frozen in the manuscript source audit at BTemu 8ff5394.

## 1. Relation to 21cmEMUv3

Reference: [21cmEMUv3, arXiv:2606.00219v1, Sections 3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.00219v1).

The reference uses diffusion for cylindrical PS and LSTM/MLP branches for other summaries, including a feed-forward optical-depth branch. Its reported mean cylindrical prediction averages 200 generated samples, at about 2 s on its GPU setup. This is a measured reference configuration, not a speed forecast for our model. Its parameterization and observable definitions differ from the current BT-history emulator.

The reference motivates treating finite-volume variance explicitly. It does not establish that diffusion is required by a generic IC noise floor or that our one-realization labels already define a verified conditional mean.

## 2. Define the scientific output before choosing an architecture

The minimum proposed extension is a precisely defined PS observable alongside the existing history approximation. Candidate later outputs remain spherical PS, mean spin temperature, global brightness temperature, LF and optical depth; each needs a scientific use and validation target. Retain the current exact LF provider and history-derived optical depth for the existing paper.

Freeze the coordinate list and priors, real/redshift-space convention, coeval versus lightcone construction, line-of-sight window, redshift selection, box/resolution, k bin edges and units, mode masks, normalization, initial conditions and postprocessing. HII_DIM and DIM have different roles; `512^3` alone does not specify the PS field resolution.

Keep three targets distinct: an individual realization, a finite-IC average, and a conditional population mean or IC-marginalized likelihood. A squared-error regressor can target a conditional mean under suitable sampling and optimization; evaluate that claim using repeat-IC references with uncertainty and independent predictor tests.

## 3. Inventory existing data, then measure a small pilot

The repository exports history summaries rather than the spatial fields required to reconstruct a PS. Inventory external archives and receipts before assuming boxes were discarded or rerunning simulations. A volume-mean xHI history alone cannot reconstruct spatial PS.

Measure the complete proposed PS/lightcone calculation, extraction and storage on a bounded pilot before committing to a database. Report CPU core-hours, peak memory, transient storage, failures and retained summaries. The existing approximately 416 s, 16-core history timing is only an arithmetic reference; lightcone and PS costs may differ.

If a future run actually cost 416 s on 16 cores, its cost would be 1.8489 core-hours. Conditional totals would be:

| Realizations | Core-hours under that assumption | Ideal days at 1000 continuously available cores |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 10,000 | 18,489 | 0.77 |
| 25,000 | 46,222 | 1.93 |
| 47,000 | 86,898 | 3.62 |

These are arithmetic estimates, excluding queueing, extraction overhead, failed attempts, training and validation. Availability of 1000 cores is not established and does not override the current project resource limits. The earlier one-to-three-week schedule is not a validated completion estimate.

For float32 maps with 32 by 64 bins, one model uses `32*64*N_z*4` bytes before metadata:

| Stored redshift windows | Bytes/model | Decimal GB for 47,000 models |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 21 | 172,032 | 8.09 |
| 31 | 253,952 | 11.94 |

Choose one redshift/window definition before budgeting. Integer redshifts 5 through 30 give 26 nodes, whereas 5 through 35 give 31; the previous 21-window storage example was not the same as its 31-node text. Neither quantity fixes the lightcone windowing convention. Do not assert a full-lightcone storage volume without its grid, fields and frequency depth.

## 4. Architecture and validation decisions

Use measured accuracy and complete-likelihood cost to select among deterministic regression and a generative model. CPU/GPU training times, memory, GPU allocation and sampling counts are to be measured, not assumed from another architecture or machine. A prediction at one redshift is not necessarily the cost of all windows and generated samples at one physical parameter point.

Freeze training, development validation and an independent final test by physical-parameter group. Measure PS errors by k, redshift and signal floor, identify masked modes, quantify repeat-IC reference uncertainty, then compare matching converged inference. A preliminary 10% development holdout alone does not qualify final-test accuracy.

Stage decisions: data inventory -> frozen observable/target -> measured pilot -> chosen architecture -> independent prediction and likelihood checks -> matching inference. No PS result or emulator extension is claimed for the present manuscript.
