\title{Blue-tilted primordial power spectra and reionization: exploratory joint inference with galaxy astrophysics}
\author{Haina Huang}
\affiliation{Department of Physics, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shatin, Hong Kong, China}
\date{November 4, 2026}
\begin{abstract}
A change in the small-scale matter spectrum can alter the predicted sources of reionization, while galaxy parameters provide additional freedom to fit the observations. We examine their interplay in a BT-modified 21cmFAST calculation with a spectral break scale $k_p$ and small-scale slope MS. The galaxy prescription contains eight astrophysical parameters; the current joint runs vary six of them, with two X-ray parameters fixed. At fixed astrophysics, the exported ionization histories separate as the spectrum changes. After fitting galaxy parameters independently for each spectrum, the stored luminosity-function maxima remain close to the PL reference over part of the displayed grid and deteriorate elsewhere. Joint samples combine luminosity functions, optical depth and a neutral-fraction constraint, and explore six galaxy coordinates or those coordinates together with $k_p$ and MS. The new six-coordinate observation-combination summaries show their largest decrease in sample spread in the escape-fraction normalization. Newer fixed-spectrum summaries also show changes in the fitted galaxy centres across spectral settings. The free-spectrum distributions are broad. Independent convergence has not been established, so weak observational information and incomplete sampling remain possible explanations. These results motivate a joint treatment of spectrum and galaxy freedom and a comparison of the information supplied by each observation.
\end{abstract}
