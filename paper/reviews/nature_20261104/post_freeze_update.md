
# Post-freeze source update

During synchronization, upstream revision `e250ae0` added four 100k-model corner PNGs for kp10/MS2.5, kp1/MS1.5, kp5/MS1.5 and fixed-kp10/free-MS 7D runs. They were received after all three reviewer reports and the forensic pass had been frozen. They were not shown to the reviewers and are not counted as independent reviewer agreement. The original reports and packet remain unchanged.

A separate visual inspection shows that the supplied figures label 3000 steps and 32 walkers and now display the t_STAR coordinate. They provide additional visual sample distributions for the newer predictor. They do not supply raw-chain retention, independent diagnostics or a definition connecting their title summaries to the previously reported centre/spread table. No new numerical estimate is extracted from raster text or contours. These figures therefore update the inventory from summary-only to summaries plus corner images, without resolving the core inference or deployed-predictor validation questions. Their incorporation into a revised results display is a separate authoring step.
