## 6. Comparison with 21cmEMUv3's validation standard

21cmEMUv3 (arXiv 2606.00219; ICML 2026 workshop) trains a hybrid diffusion (2D 21-cm PS) + LSTM
(six summary observables) emulator on ~47k lightcone realizations (400 cMpc, 1.5 cMpc/cell, z=4-35),
each with its own initial conditions, and reports sub-percent median fractional accuracy on a
held-out test set (per-band PS errors of order 1.5-3.8%, ~0.55% averaged over wavenumbers).
Their downstream evidence is an inference comparison against 21cmFAST; they do not impose R-hat/ESS
chain gates or formal tau/xHI quantile thresholds.

Our current precision (65k Direct ResMLP, worst of five seeds):

| Metric | Value | Unit |
|---|---:|---|
| xHI trajectory RMSE median | 0.0010 (0.10%) | absolute (physical) |
| xHI trajectory RMSE q95 | 0.0101 | absolute |
| xHI(5.9) absolute error q90 | 0.0079 | absolute |
| tau absolute error q90 | 0.00070 | absolute |
| Delta logL_joint q90 | 2.44 | likelihood level (single-IC noise floor 0.948) |
| Posterior centre shift vs exact chain | < 0.25 sigma | relative to exact posterior width |

Interpretation: on the "median fractional error" yardstick our 0.10% absolute xHI error (roughly
0.3-1% fractional in the transition region) is at or better than the sub-percent level reported by
21cmEMUv3. Our acceptance is stricter: we additionally report tau/xHI quantiles, a likelihood-level
Delta logL, posterior consistency, and chain convergence. The Delta logL <= 0.1 target is unreachable
with single-IC labels (noise floor 0.948), whereas the posterior-consistency test already passes at
the < 0.25 sigma level.
