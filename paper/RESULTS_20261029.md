RMSE = 0.0010 is a dimensionless absolute error. Comparisons with published fractional-error metrics require matching the observable, parameter domain, and validation design; the exported numbers alone do not define such a comparison.

The completed [IC audit](../docs/ic_audit_completion.md) uses 128 parameter families with eight realizations per family. Its value **0.9480653** is the across-family q90 of the within-family sample standard deviation of the history log likelihood. It measures realization scatter and is distinct from emulator-error q90. The q90 absolute difference between log likelihood of the finite-IC mean history and log of the finite-IC mean likelihood is **0.308246**. These measurements do not establish that a 0.1 emulator-error threshold is unattainable, change an acceptance criterion, or establish posterior fidelity.
