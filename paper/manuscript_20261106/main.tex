\title{BTemu: a history emulator for BT-modified reionization calculations}
\author{Haina Huang}
\affiliation{Department of Physics, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shatin, Hong Kong, China}
\date{November 6, 2026}
\begin{abstract}
Small-scale matter spectra and galaxy formation both affect reionization, so spectral inference requires marginalization over uncertain galaxy properties. We use BTemu to predict 32-node neutral-fraction histories from eight galaxy and heating coordinates, a matter-spectrum break scale $k_p$ and a small-scale slope $m_s$. The luminosity-function calculation remains exact. The 100,000-history model reports trajectory-RMSE median 0.000850668 and 95th percentile 0.00556364 in its development evaluation. A representative PL/BT comparison shows changes in the selected galaxy distributions. We then vary six galaxy parameters together with $k_p,m_s$ and compare their marginalized distributions with uniform-prior references. The last 300 steps of the uploaded 600-step chain give 95\% sample intervals $k_p\in[4.420,29.071]\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$ and $m_s\in[0.584,3.962]$. These preliminary distributions show little range reduction for $m_s$ and a change at low $k_p$. Independent sampling and the current model's validation arrays are needed to establish the final observational constraints.
\end{abstract}
